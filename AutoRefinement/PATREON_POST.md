# Patreon post draft — Auto Refinement

**Title:** Auto Refinement — refineries that run without a Duplicant (new mod!)

---

New Oxygen Not Included mod: **Auto Refinement**. ⚙️

Refining coal into refined carbon, crushing rock, forging glass — all of it
needs a Duplicant standing there doing nothing interesting while the machine does
the work. An Auto-Sweeper can already feed the building and take the product
away. The one thing it can't do is press the button.

Here's the thing I found while digging into it: **the game already supports
this.** `ComplexFabricator` carries a "no operator needed" switch, and seven
vanilla buildings already use it — the Kiln, Rubber Maker, Smoker, Chlorinator,
Data Miner, Food Dehydrator and Uranium Centrifuge all run on their own. Auto
Refinement extends that to everything else.

- ✅ **Metal Refinery, Rock Crusher, Glass Forge, Supermaterial Refinery**,
  presses, kilns, cooking stations, apothecaries — and buildings added by
  **other mods**, picked up automatically
- ✅ **Duplicants can still help.** By default the errand stays, so someone may
  wander over and work it — it just isn't required. Normal speed on its own,
  **half again as fast** with a Duplicant, Machinery skill on top. Both rates
  configurable from 1% to 500%
- ✅ Or turn the errand off entirely for **silent automation** — Duplicants
  ignore the building completely
- ✅ **The Desalinator empties itself**, dropping its salt where a Duplicant
  would have left it so an Auto-Sweeper still collects it
- ✅ **Mod Options** groups everything into categories — refinement, cooking,
  medicine, clothing, crafting, research — so you automate some and leave
  others manual
- ✅ **Nothing is written to your save.** Existing colonies work, and
  uninstalling leaves no trace

**Fully standalone — no other mods required.**
